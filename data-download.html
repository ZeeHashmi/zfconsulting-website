Data Requests

You can request a copy of personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or request deletion where applicable.

How to request your data

Email info@zfconsulting.com.au with the subject “Data request” and include enough detail for us to identify your enquiry. Please do not send passwords or sensitive identity documents unless we specifically request a secure method.

What you may receive

Depending on your request, we may provide information you submitted through email or other direct contact. The browser self-check does not require an account and is designed not to send a personal report to us.

We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. We aim to respond within a reasonable period.
