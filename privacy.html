Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

ZF Consulting Services Pty Ltd (ABN 30 154 539 438) respects your privacy. This website provides information about consulting services and free computer-check resources.

Information we may receive

If you contact us by email or phone, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, contact details and message. The browser self-check is designed to run locally in your browser and does not require an account.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, provide requested services, maintain the website and improve its security. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use this website, including which pages are viewed and general browser, device and approximate location information. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and may process information on Google's behalf, including outside Australia. We use this information to understand website traffic and improve the site; we do not use Google Analytics to identify your exact address. You can limit or remove cookies in your browser settings. For details about Google's data practices, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Zee AI web demonstration

When the interactive Zee AI demonstration is available, the new message you submit and recent conversation context are sent to OpenAI to generate a reply. Do not submit confidential, sensitive or personal information. ZF Consulting Services does not store chat content in its Supabase database. Before showing the chat or accepting a message, the service uses the country code supplied by the Supabase gateway in the cf-ipcountry request header when available; it does not accept a country code from the browser. If that header is missing or unknown, the service sends the gateway-provided client IP to ipapi.co for a country lookup. Supabase and, for this fallback lookup, ipapi.co process the IP address under their respective terms and privacy practices. Visitors identified outside Australia are not shown the chat. To prevent repeat access attempts, a restricted database stores a keyed, pseudonymous hash of the IP address and country code with a 30-day expiry; expired records are removed during a later access check or when a new block is recorded. ZF Consulting Services does not store the raw address in this block list. The service also enforces a limit of 10 successful messages per browser and IP address per UTC day using keyed hashes and daily request counts; prior-day counters are deleted during a later valid demo request. OpenAI may process submitted messages under its own terms and privacy practices.

PayPal contributions

If you choose to make a voluntary contribution, you will be redirected to PayPal to enter an amount and complete payment. PayPal processes payment details under its own terms and privacy practices; ZF Consulting Services does not collect card or bank details through this website. Contributions are not tax-deductible charitable donations.
