What is penetration testing?

Penetration testing simulates realistic attacks using controlled techniques to identify vulnerabilities in your systems, applications, networks and processes. The outcome is a clear view of risk, evidence to support remediation and recommendations prioritised for your business.

Understanding your risk

Modern businesses depend on connected systems, cloud services and trusted users. A weakness in any one of these areas can expose sensitive information or disrupt operations. Testing your defences helps you understand which risks matter most and where to invest effort first.
